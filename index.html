QR Label Studio

Turn a CSV of URLs into a printable QR-code sticker sheet that lines up with your label template. Everything runs locally in your browser — your files never leave your device.

1. Upload your label template

A PDF where each sticker cell is outlined (rectangles or rounded rectangles). We read the outlines to figure out the grid.

2. Upload a CSV of URLs

One URL per line (first column). Blank lines are skipped.

3. Label text

This text appears on every sticker, to the right of the QR code.

4. Generate

Download qr_labels.pdf

Built with pdf-lib, pdfjs-dist & qrcode. Page 1 of the output is a print-instructions sheet; pages 2+ contain the stickers.
